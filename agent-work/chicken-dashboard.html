Notes & caveats
  • “Chickens” = FAO “stocks”: birds alive at a single point in time (item “Chickens”, not all poultry). Because broilers live only ~6 weeks, this understates annual production.
  • Data years vary (2020–2024). Germany, Italy, Spain, Ireland and a few others have not reported a recent FAO figure; their latest available year is shown.
  • Exceptions: only Iceland and Luxembourg have more people than chickens. Microstates (Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, Liechtenstein, Vatican) and Kosovo are excluded for lack of reported commercial flocks.
  • Human populations are approximate mid-2020s figures for context; the ratio is what matters here.
  • Sources: FAO FAOSTAT “Crops and Livestock Products” (Chickens, stocks), via atlasbig.com / invokely.ai / Our World in Data.
  • Base map: Europe SVG (Robinson projection, ISO-coded) by simplemaps.com — free for commercial use, attribution appreciated.
